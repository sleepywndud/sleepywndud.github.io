## 2026-11-08 Exam Season
To those who has made it this far, you have done well.

Whatever you get as the result, the time you have working hard was not for nothing.

Don't be worried for the exam!

Whenever you see something right in front of you, 
it seems like the biggest thing ever.

But looking back, it always turns out to be something small,
something so small that you don't even remember it fully. 

Just calmly show what you've prepared for all this time, as it is.

On the night before the exam, let go of all the tension and stress for a moment, and tell yourself, 

"You truly did well."

Thank you for using NBTS this year.
I hope to see you again!

~ JY.
