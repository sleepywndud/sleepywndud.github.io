Half-angle identities

$$
\sin \frac{\theta}{2}=\pm \sqrt{ \frac{1-\cos \theta}{2} }
$$


$$
\cos \frac{\theta}{2}=\pm \sqrt{ \frac{1+\cos \theta}{2} }
$$


$$
\tan \frac{\theta}{2}= \frac{1-\cos \theta}{\sin \theta }
$$

These formulas are derived from the *cosine double angle* formula.

### Sine Half-Angle:

$$
\cos 2x=1-2\sin ^{2}x
$$


$$
2\sin ^{2}x=1-\cos 2x
$$


$$
\sin ^{2}x=\frac{1-\cos 2x}{2}
$$


$$
\sin x=\pm \sqrt{ \frac{1-\cos 2x}{2} }
$$

Let $x=\frac{\theta}{2}$, so $2x=\theta$:

$$
\sin \frac{\theta}{2}=\pm \sqrt{ \frac{1-\cos \theta}{2} }
$$


### Cosine Half-Angle

$$
\cos 2x=2\cos ^{2}x-1
$$


$$
2\cos ^{2}x=1+\cos 2x
$$


$$
\cos ^{2}x=\frac{1+\cos 2x}{2}
$$


$$
\cos x=\pm \sqrt{ \frac{1+\cos 2x}{2} }
$$

Let $x=\frac{\theta}{2}$:

$$
\cos \frac{\theta}{2}=\pm \sqrt{ \frac{1+\cos \theta}{2} }
$$


### Tangent Half-Angle

$$
\tan \frac{\theta}{2}=\frac{\sin \frac{\theta}{2}}{\cos \frac{\theta}{2}}
$$

Multiply top and bottom by $2\sin \frac{\theta}{2}$:

$$
\tan \frac{\theta}{2}=\frac{2\sin ^{2}\frac{\theta}{2}}{2\sin \frac{\theta}{2}\cos \frac{\theta}{2}}
$$

From the sine working above, $2\sin ^{2}\frac{\theta}{2}=1-\cos \theta$.
From the sine double angle formula, $2\sin \frac{\theta}{2}\cos \frac{\theta}{2}=\sin \theta$.

$$
\tan \frac{\theta}{2}=\frac{1-\cos \theta}{\sin \theta}
$$
