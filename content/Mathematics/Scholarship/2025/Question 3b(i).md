$$
\tan \left( 3 \cdot \frac{\pi}{8} \right)=\tan\left( \frac{\pi}{8}\cdot (2+1) \right)=\tan\left( \frac{2\pi}{8}+\frac{\pi}{8} \right)
$$

Using compound angles:

$$
\tan\left( \frac{2\pi}{8}+\frac{\pi}{8} \right)= \frac{ \tan \frac{2\pi}{8}+\tan \frac{\pi}{8}}{1-\tan \frac{2\pi}{8} \cdot \tan \frac{\pi}{8}}
$$


$$
= \frac{ \tan\left( 2 \cdot \frac{\pi}{8} \right)+\tan \frac{\pi}{8}}{1-\tan \left( 2 \cdot \frac{\pi}{8} \right)\cdot \tan \frac{\pi}{8}}
$$


$$
=\frac{1+\tan \frac{\pi}{8}}{1-1\cdot \tan \frac{\pi}{8}}
$$

Since $\tan \frac{\pi}{8}=\sqrt{ 2 }-1$:

$$
=\frac{1+\sqrt{ 2 }-1}{1-(\sqrt{ 2 }-1)}
$$


$$
=\frac{\sqrt{ 2 }}{2-\sqrt{ 2 }}
$$


$$
=1+\sqrt{ 2 }
$$
