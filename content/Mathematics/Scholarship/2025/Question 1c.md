How many solutions?

$$
z^{2025}=\overline{z}
$$

Multiply both sides by $z$

$$
z^{2026}=\underbrace{ z\overline{z} }_{ \text{Constant} }
$$

FTA states that LHS has 2026 solutions since LHS is a complex equation and RHS is a constant.

Also, another solution can occur when $z=0 \implies \overline{z}=0$.

In conclusion, 2027 solutions. #
