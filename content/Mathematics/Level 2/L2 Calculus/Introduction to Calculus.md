#L2MAEL 

---

L2 Calculus involves the basic 'differentiation' and 'integration (antidifferentiation)'. 

Differentiation in VERY SIMPLE terms, is **gradient**, and integration (antidifferentiation) in VERY simple terms is the **area**, or **reverse differentiation**.

Within differentiation and integration, there are subtopics involved like 'kinematics' and 'optimization'.
These subtopics involve using differentiation and/or integration depending on the situation.
