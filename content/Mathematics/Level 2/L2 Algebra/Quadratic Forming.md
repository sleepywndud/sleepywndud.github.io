#L2MAEL 

---

Quadratic forming questions are almost guaranteed in the exam.\
These types of questions consist of first forming a quadratic equation either in the intercept form or the vertex form, then answering the question.

The most common forms used are the vertex form and the intercept form.

VERTEX FORM:
$$y=a(x-p)^{2}+q$$
where $(p,q)$ is the coordinates of the vertex.

INTERCEPT FORM:
$$y=a(x-p)(x-q)$$
where $(p,0)$ and $(q,0)$ are the coordinates of the $x$-intercepts.

$a$ is the scale factor for both forms.

---

Use the VERTEX FORM when:
- Can see a **clear** vertex coordinate

Use the INTERCEPT FORM when:
- Can see clear $x$-intercept coordinates

If you can use either, use the vertex form -- it's easier.

---

e.g. If you can see a clear vertex at $(2,1)$, then the equation would be:
$$y=a(x-2)^{2}+1$$
$a$ can be calculated by finding another coordinate that passes the graph. 
However, **YOU CANNOT USE** the same coordinate that you used. e.g. if you used the vertex form, you CANNOT USE the coordinates of the vertex to find $a$. Same applies for the intercept form.

---

Once a quadratic equation is formed, answer the question appropriately!
