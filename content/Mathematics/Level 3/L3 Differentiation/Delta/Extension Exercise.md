**10-3a,b)**

**a)** Let $g(x)$ be a twice-differentiable function which satisfies this condition: 

$g'(x)=0$ implies $g''(x)\neq 0$ for all values of $x$.

If $h(x)=e^{g(x)}$, find $h''(x)$ and prove that $g(x)$ and $h(x)$ take local maximum values at exactly the same points.

**b)** Verify that if $g(x)=x+2\sin (x)$, then $g'(x)=0$ implies $g''(x)\neq 0$. Hence (or otherwise) find the points in the interval $-2\pi<x<2\pi$ at which $h(x)=e^{x+2\sin(x)}$ takes local maximum values.

**12-a,b,c,d)**
**a)** A tangent to the curve of $y=x^{2}-2x+3$ passes through the point $(4,7)$. Find the point(s) where the tangent touches the curve.

**b)** One of the points on the graph of $y=3x^{2}+4$ can be written as $(a,b)$, where $a>0$. The tangent at $(a,b)$ makes an angle of $45^o$ with each axis. Find where the tangent cuts the $x$-axis.

**c)** $f(x)=ax^{2}+bx+c$ has equal and real roots at points $P$ and $Q$, where $P=(p,0)$ and $Q=(q,0)$.
Show that the normals to the parabola at $P$ and $Q$ intersect on the line:
$$
x=\frac{p+q}{2}
$$

**d)** A tangent to the curve $y=x^{2}+4$ passes through the point $(-2,7)$. Find the equation(s) of this tangent.
