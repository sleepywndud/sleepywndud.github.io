Hookes Law, is one equation:
$$F=-kx$$
which calculates the *force* from an elastic object like springs, or rubber bands. 

$k$ is the spring constant (this tells you how strong/weak the spring is).\
$x$ is the distance of *how far the spring extended*.

The calculations involving the equation is often achieved. 

However, equations involving the formula:
$$E_{p}=\frac{1}{2}kx^{2}$$
which calculates the *energy* from an elastic object, is often an M/E level calculation.

The calculations are so simple.
