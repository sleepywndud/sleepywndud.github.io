- $T \uparrow, K_{c} \uparrow \implies \overrightarrow{\text{Endothermic}}$
- $T\uparrow, K_{c}\downarrow \implies \overrightarrow{\text{Exothermic}}$
- $T\downarrow, K_{c}\uparrow \implies \overrightarrow{\text{Exothermic}}$
- $T\downarrow, K_{c}\downarrow\implies \overrightarrow{\text{Endothermic}}$ 
...where $T$ indicates *temperature*, $K_{c}$ is the equilibrium constant $\frac{[P]}{[R]}$, and $\overrightarrow{\text{Endo/Exo}}$ is what the *forward reaction* is.


Let's prove the first rule where $T\uparrow, K_{c}\uparrow \implies \overrightarrow{\text{Endothermic}}$.

Proof:\
Le Chat's principle states that if a dynamic equilibrium reaction is applied stress (heat, more reactants/products, etc..) the system tries to recover its equilibrium by shifting the equilibrium position, and establishing a new equilibrium point.

$K_{c}$, the equilibrium constant is defined by

$$
K_{c}=\frac{[P]}{[R]}
$$

For $K_{c}$ to increase, the concentration of the products must increase.\
And for the products to increase, then there must be some factor -- which is heat -- that makes it so that the system produces more products. 

This is where Le Chat's principle comes in. 

Let's say we have a dynamic equilibrium system of

$$
R \rightleftharpoons P
$$

We need to make more products, and to do that, stress must be added to the reactant side, which then according to Le Chat's principle, the system would favor the forwards reaction ultimately making more products. In this case, the 'stress' would be heat (marked as $T$ in the reaction).

$$
\overrightarrow{R+T \rightleftharpoons} \ P
$$

If more products are created, then $K_{c}$ increases, by the definition of the equilibrium constant.\
The same proof applies to the other rules up the top.

---

1. When temperature is increased/decreased,
2. The dynamic equilibrium system tries to minimize the effect of the change by absorbing/releasing heat.
3. Since there is an increase/decrease in $K_{c}$,
4. This indicates that the forward reaction is exothermic/endothermic.
5. Hence more reactants/products is formed, as the system favors the forwards/reverse reactions.
